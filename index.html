A structured, adaptive method

From initial assessment to continuity.

Each engagement is organized around defined objectives, scope, responsibilities and agreed deliverables.

  1. 01

    Initial assessment

    Define the challenge, objectives, operating environment, constraints, stakeholders, available resources and desired outcomes. Establish the initial scope and determine what information or expertise is required.

  2. 02

    Strategic framework

    Translate the initial assessment into a strategic framework. Identify priorities, critical questions, decision points, potential courses of action and the conditions that may affect execution.

  3. 03

    Project design and scope

    Establish the project architecture, including scope, workstreams, responsibilities, milestones, timelines, deliverables, decision authorities and appropriate measures of progress.

  4. 04

    Research and due diligence

    Gather and evaluate relevant information, conduct research and analysis, examine assumptions, identify material risks and opportunities, and develop an evidence-based understanding of the challenge.

  5. 05

    Coordination and execution

    Coordinate approved workstreams, participants, specialists and external resources. Manage priorities, dependencies, timelines, communications and emerging issues while maintaining alignment with the engagement objectives.

  6. 06

    Monitoring and adaptation

    Monitor progress against agreed objectives, milestones and deliverables. Assess changing circumstances, new information, risks and dependencies, adapting the project approach when necessary.

  7. 07

    Review and delivery

    Conduct structured reviews of findings, work products and outcomes. Refine and validate deliverables before presenting final recommendations, analysis, plans or other agreed outputs.

  8. 08

    Closeout and continuity

    Complete the agreed engagement, document key findings and decisions, identify outstanding matters, and establish appropriate next steps, follow-on work or continuing advisory support where applicable.

Engagement principles

Structured. Defined objectives, scope, responsibilities and deliverables.

Discreet. Information and engagement details receive appropriate professional discretion.

Adaptive. The methodology can adjust as information, priorities or circumstances emerge.

Outcome-focused. Work is directed toward useful analysis, decisions, plans or other agreed results.

How an engagement is scoped and proposed

Once a matter has been sufficiently understood, DRGS may develop a written proposal defining the appropriate scope, structure and expected outcomes.

  1. 01 Assessment — understand the matter
  2. 02 Scope — define the work
  3. 03 Proposal — establish the engagement
  4. 04 Review — discuss scope and terms
  5. 05 Agreement — confirm the engagement
  6. 06 Onboarding — begin the work
  7. 07 Execution — conduct the work
  8. 08 Delivery and continuity — review and determine next steps

04 About DRGS

Rooted in Puerto Rico. Connected to a wider world.

Del Rio Global Strategies is a strategic consulting and foresight organization focused on Puerto Rico, the Americas and the Caribbean.

Its work encompasses business strategy, economic development, infrastructure, energy, digital transformation, strategic communications, investment and cross-border initiatives.

The organization develops strategic research, analysis, planning frameworks and advisory perspectives addressing complex economic, technological, infrastructure and regional-development challenges.

From Puerto Rico, DRGS examines developments across the Americas and Caribbean. Many modern challenges extend beyond individual jurisdictions and require connections between markets, institutions, infrastructure, technology and communities.

An interdisciplinary approach

Complex challenges rarely fit within a single discipline. Economic, institutional, technological, infrastructure, energy and regional perspectives help develop a more complete understanding of the challenge and inform practical strategies for long-term resilience and sustainable development.

Questions about publications, custom research or the checkout process.

How do I receive an investment outlook?

Are custom research briefs instant downloads?

No. Custom briefs are prepared for you after checkout, review of your research details and confirmation of scope. The completed personalized PDF is delivered by email.

No. Financial scenarios use illustrative assumptions to support evaluation and are not forecasts or guarantees of returns. Actual outcomes depend on project-specific assumptions, market conditions and execution.
