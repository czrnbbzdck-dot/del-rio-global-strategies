01 — About

Understanding complexity. Building strategy.

Del Rio Global Strategies is a strategic consulting and foresight organization focused on Puerto Rico, the Americas, and the Caribbean.

Its work encompasses business strategy, economic development, infrastructure, energy, digital transformation, strategic communications, investment, and cross-border initiatives.

The organization develops strategic research, analysis, planning frameworks, and advisory perspectives addressing complex economic, technological, infrastructure, and regional-development challenges.

A regional perspective

Del Rio Global Strategies draws on economic, institutional, technological, and regional perspectives to examine emerging challenges, identify opportunities, and develop practical strategies for long-term resilience and sustainable development.

AN INTERDISCIPLINARY APPROACH

Complex challenges rarely fit within a single discipline. Del Rio Global Strategies brings together economic, institutional, technological, infrastructure, energy, and regional perspectives to develop a more complete understanding of the challenge.

CONSULTING & PROJECT MANAGEMENT APPROACH

01 — INITIAL ASSESSMENT

Define the challenge, objectives, operating environment, constraints, stakeholders, available resources, and desired outcomes. Establish the initial scope and determine what information or expertise is required.

02 — STRATEGIC FRAMEWORK

Translate the initial assessment into a strategic framework. Identify priorities, critical questions, decision points, potential courses of action, and the conditions that may affect execution.

03 — PROJECT DESIGN & SCOPE

Establish the project architecture, including scope, workstreams, responsibilities, milestones, timelines, deliverables, decision authorities, and appropriate measures of progress.

04 — RESEARCH, ANALYSIS & DUE DILIGENCE

Gather and evaluate relevant information, conduct research and analysis, examine assumptions, identify material risks and opportunities, and develop an evidence-based understanding of the challenge.

05 — COORDINATION & EXECUTION

Coordinate approved workstreams, participants, specialists, and external resources. Manage priorities, dependencies, timelines, communications, and emerging issues while maintaining alignment with the engagement objectives.

06 — MONITORING & ADAPTATION

Monitor progress against agreed objectives, milestones, and deliverables. Assess changing circumstances, new information, risks, and dependencies, adapting the project approach when necessary.

07 — REVIEW & DELIVERY

Conduct structured reviews of findings, work products, and outcomes. Refine and validate deliverables before presenting final recommendations, analysis, plans, or other agreed outputs.

08 — CLOSEOUT & CONTINUITY

Complete the agreed engagement, document key findings and decisions, identify outstanding matters, and establish appropriate next steps, follow-on work, or continuing advisory support where applicable.

ENGAGEMENT PRINCIPLES

Structured. Each engagement is organized around defined objectives, scope, responsibilities, and deliverables.

Discreet. Information, relationships, and engagement details are handled with appropriate professional discretion.

Adaptive. The methodology can adjust as new information, priorities, or circumstances emerge.

Outcome-focused. The objective is to produce useful analysis, decisions, plans, or other clearly defined results.
